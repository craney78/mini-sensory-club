Sensory Play

Supervision, Safety and Suitability

Mini Sensory Club creates playful, engaging and thoughtful sensory experiences for babies, toddlers and young children. Each child is different, and families should use their own judgment about suitability.

👶

Parent and Carer Supervision

Children attending Mini Sensory Club activities must be supervised by a parent, carer, educator or responsible adult at all times.

🧸

Age and Development

Activities are designed with babies, toddlers and young children in mind, but every child develops at their own pace. Families and centres are responsible for deciding whether an activity is suitable for each child.

🌿

Allergies and Sensitivities

Families, carers and centres should tell Mini Sensory Club about any known allergies, sensitivities, dietary concerns or medical considerations before a child participates.

Important Note

Not Medical, Therapeutic or Developmental Advice

The sensory play information shared on this website is general in nature. It is not intended to replace advice from a doctor, allied health professional, therapist, early intervention provider or other qualified professional.

If you have concerns about your child’s health, development, sensory needs, allergies or participation, please seek advice from an appropriate professional.

💛

Gentle, Play-Based Experiences

Mini Sensory Club is designed to provide warm, playful and welcoming sensory experiences. We do not diagnose, assess or provide therapy services through this website or our general play sessions.
