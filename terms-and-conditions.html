Weekly Sensory Classes

Class Terms

Mini Sensory Club weekly classes are 45 minute sensory play sessions held on Mondays and Fridays, commencing at 9:30am and 10:30am.

Classes are primarily designed for babies aged 4 to 12 months, with a tiny baby corner for babies aged 4 to 6 months. The Friday 10:30am class is open to children aged 0 to 5 years.

Class capacity is limited to support a calm, boutique and welcoming environment. Families are responsible for supervising their child during each session.

Childcare Centre Incursions

Incursion Terms

Mini Sensory Club childcare centre incursions are designed for children aged 0 to 2 years and include a large play mat, three themed sensory trays, supplied equipment, setup and pack down.

Both Mini Sensory Club educators attend incursions. Centres remain responsible for child supervision, ratios, centre policies, room safety and any child-specific medical, dietary, allergy or support information.

Incursion themes, materials and activities may vary depending on availability, season, centre requirements and suitability for the children attending.
