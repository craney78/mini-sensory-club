Our Story

Created from Kindness, Family Values and a Love of Learning

Mini Sensory Club is more than a baby and toddler activity. It is a warm, family-led business created by two sisters who care deeply about children, families and meaningful early learning experiences.

Ruby and Arabella have grown up with strong family values, kindness and a natural care for others. That warmth is at the centre of Mini Sensory Club and can be felt in the way every class, incursion and party experience is planned.

Their approach is real-life over polished, playful without feeling overwhelming, and professional while still feeling personal and welcoming.
