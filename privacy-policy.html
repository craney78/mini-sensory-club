Privacy Policy

Our Approach

Privacy Matters to Us

Mini Sensory Club is a sisterhood-led sensory play business based in Newcastle, NSW. We aim to keep our communication with families and centres clear, respectful and easy to understand.

💛

Who This Policy Applies To

This privacy policy applies to visitors using this website, families making enquiries or bookings, parents and carers attending classes, childcare centres booking incursions and customers arranging soft play party hire.

🌈

Why We Collect Information

We only collect information that helps us respond to enquiries, manage bookings, provide safe and suitable sensory play experiences and communicate with families, carers and centres.

Personal Information

Information We May Collect

The type of information collected may depend on whether you are making a general enquiry, booking a class, arranging an incursion or organising party hire.

👩‍👧

Family and Contact Details

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, suburb, enquiry details and preferred way to be contacted.

👶

Child Information

For classes, incursions or party hire, we may collect a child’s first name, age, session details and information that helps us support safe participation.

🌿

Safety and Suitability

We may collect information about allergies, sensitivities, medical considerations, access needs or other details you choose to share so we can plan appropriately.

📅

Booking Information

We may collect details about booking dates, session times, party hire packages, incursion requirements, venue details, attendance numbers and payment status.

💬

Messages and Enquiries

We may keep records of messages sent through the website contact form, email, Instagram, Humanitix or other communication channels.

💻

Website Information

This website may collect basic technical information, such as browser type, device information, pages visited and general website performance information.

Collection

How We Collect Information

Most personal information is collected directly from you when you choose to contact Mini Sensory Club, complete an enquiry form, email us, message us on Instagram, book a class, arrange an incursion or organise soft play party hire.

We may also receive booking-related information through Humanitix or another booking platform used by Mini Sensory Club.

For childcare centre incursions, information may be provided by the centre, educator, director or authorised contact person responsible for the booking.

Use of Information

How We Use Personal Information

Personal information helps Mini Sensory Club provide clear communication, safe planning and warm service for families, carers, centres and event hosts.

💌

Responding to Enquiries

We use contact details and enquiry information to respond to questions about weekly classes, childcare centre incursions, party hire, pricing and availability.

📅

Managing Bookings

We use booking information to organise classes, confirm session details, manage attendance, prepare activities and communicate any updates.

🧸

Providing Services

We use information to plan sensory trays, class themes, incursion setups and party hire arrangements that suit the age and needs of the children attending.

🌿

Safety and Suitability

Allergy, sensitivity, medical or access information may be used to help us make safe and thoughtful decisions about materials, activities and setup.

✨

Service Improvement

Feedback and general enquiry trends may help us improve our classes, incursions, party hire packages, communication and website experience.

📝

Records and Administration

We may use information for basic business records, payment records, insurance, safety, legal, accounting or administrative purposes.

📷

Photos and Social Media

Mini Sensory Club may take or use photos of classes, incursions, setups or events only where appropriate consent has been provided.

Photos may be used on the website, Instagram, marketing materials or other Mini Sensory Club communication channels where consent allows.

Children’s Privacy

Protecting Children’s Information

Mini Sensory Club works with babies, toddlers and young children. Any child-related information is treated with care and is only collected where it helps us manage bookings, support participation, plan activities or provide safer sensory play experiences.

Parents, carers, guardians, childcare centres or authorised adults are responsible for providing accurate child-related information when making a booking or enquiry.

Disclosure

When Information May Be Shared

Mini Sensory Club does not sell personal information. Information may only be shared where it is needed to run the business, provide services, meet legal obligations or protect safety.

🎟️

Booking Platforms

Weekly class bookings may be processed through Humanitix or another booking platform. These platforms may collect and manage booking and payment information under their own privacy policies.

💻

Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers for website hosting, email, forms, booking systems, payment processing, accounting, administration or business support.

🛡️

Safety or Legal Reasons

Information may be shared where required by law, where needed for insurance, safety, dispute handling or where necessary to protect children, families, staff, centres or equipment.

Platforms

Third-Party Websites and Overseas Storage

Some service providers may store or process information in Australia or overseas, depending on how their systems are operated. Where possible, families and centres should review the privacy information provided by those platforms when using them.

🔗

External Links

Mini Sensory Club is not responsible for the content, privacy practices, security or availability of external websites or platforms that are linked from this website.
