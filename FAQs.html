Weekly Classes

Questions About Sensory Classes

Our weekly classes are warm, boutique sensory play sessions designed for babies, toddlers and young children.

How long does each class go for?

Each Mini Sensory Club class runs for 45 minutes. There is also a 15 minute changeover between sessions.

What days are classes held?

Weekly sensory classes are held on Mondays and Fridays, with sessions at 9:30am and 10:30am.

What age are the weekly classes for?

Classes are primarily designed for babies aged 4–12 months. There is also a tiny baby corner for babies aged 4–6 months.

Is there a class for older children or siblings?

Yes. The Friday 10:30am class is open to children aged 0–5 years, making it a flexible option for families with siblings or older little learners.

How many babies are in each class?

Each class has a maximum of 16 babies, helping the space feel boutique, calm and welcoming.

What themes do you use?

Themes may include Ocean, Transport, Colours, Farm Animals, Music, Nature, Bugs and Seasons.

Childcare Centre Incursions

Questions for Early Learning Centres

Mini Sensory Club offers EYLF-aligned sensory play incursions for babies and toddlers in childcare and early learning settings.

What age group are incursions designed for?

Childcare centre incursions are designed for children aged 0–2 years.

What is included in an incursion?

Each standard incursion includes a large play mat, three themed sensory trays, equipment, setup and pack down.

Do Ruby and Arabella attend the session?

Yes. Both educators are onsite for centre incursions.

Are the incursions EYLF aligned?

Yes. The experiences are designed to support sensory exploration, fine motor skills, hands-on discovery and early learning outcomes.

How much does a childcare incursion cost?

A single session is $350. Additional trays are $35 each, and an additional back-to-back session is $120.

Do you travel outside Newcastle and Lake Macquarie?

Travel outside Newcastle and Lake Macquarie may be available and is priced on application.

Soft Play Party Hire

Questions About Party Hire

Our soft play party hire packages are designed to make birthdays and celebrations playful, beautiful and easy for families.

What area do you deliver to?

Delivery, setup and pack down are included within 30 km of Whitebridge.

What is included in party hire?

This depends on the package selected. Packages may include soft play steps, climbing frame, ball pit, sensory trays and bouncy castle.

What party hire packages are available?

Mini Sensory Club offers The Mini, The Classic and The Full Setup party hire packages.

How much is soft play party hire?

The Mini is $200, The Classic is $300 and The Full Setup is $400.

Do you set everything up?

Yes. Delivery, setup and pack down are included for bookings within the service area.

How do I enquire about a party booking?

Send through your event date, suburb, party time and preferred package through the contact page.
