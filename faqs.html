Weekly Classes

Questions About Sensory Classes

Our weekly classes are warm, boutique sensory play sessions designed for babies, toddlers and young children.

How long does each class go for?

Each Mini Sensory Club class runs for 45 minutes. There is also a 15 minute changeover between sessions.

What days are classes held?

Classes run on Mondays at 10:30am and 11:30am, and on Fridays at 9:30am and 10:30am.

What age are the weekly classes for?

Monday 10:30am: 0–12 months
Monday 11:30am: 0–2 years
Friday 9:30am: 0–12 months
Friday 10:30am: 0–5 years

Is there a class for older children or siblings?

Yes. The Monday 11:30am class is for children up to 2 years, and the Friday 10:30am class is open to children aged 0–5 years, a great option for families with siblings.

How many babies are in each class?

Each class has 10–12 little ones, helping the space feel boutique, calm and welcoming.

How do I book?

All classes are booked online through linktr.ee/minisensoryclub.
